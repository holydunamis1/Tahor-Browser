The ad-blocking rules in Tahor are converted from these community lists:

Credit: The EasyList authors, https://easylist.to/. The lists are maintained by
their community; thank you to everyone who contributes to them.

## What was changed

Tahor does not use the lists as they are. `tools/blocklist/convert_abp_to_canonical.dart`
converts them into Tahor's own line format (see `docs/BLUEPRINT.md`, section 4),
keeping the rules that Android and iPhone can both apply and dropping the rest.
The rules kept are domain and path blocks, third-party and resource-type
options, `domain=` options, exception rules, and plain element-hiding rules.
The rules dropped are regular expressions, scriptlets, redirects, `csp`,
`removeparam` and other options the web views cannot honour, extended
selectors, and cosmetic exceptions. The converter counts every dropped rule by
reason each time it runs.

## How it is published

The converted list is published under the same terms as the originals, at
`<website>/blocklist/latest.txt`, signed with an Ed25519 key whose public half
is `update_public_key.txt`. A smaller starter copy ships inside the app
(`canonical_blocklist.txt`); the full list is downloaded once on first launch.
Both are generated by the `publish-blocklist` workflow and are never edited by
hand.
